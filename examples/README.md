# Worked examples

These examples show the skill's decisions, not just polished outputs. Each case records the protected facts, the diagnosis, the revision, and what the revision deliberately leaves alone.

All people, studies, organizations, and numbers below are synthetic and were created for demonstration. No unpublished manuscript or patient material is included.

| Case | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- |
| [English academic paragraph](01-english-academic.md) | Tightens an evidence-limited result without strengthening causality |
| [English workplace email](02-english-email.md) | Replaces padded politeness with a clear ask and deadline |
| [Chinese medical discussion](03-chinese-medical.md) | Preserves effect size, uncertainty, and retrospective-design limits |
| [Chinese workplace email](04-chinese-workplace-email.md) | Keeps professional tone while removing procedural filler |
| [Long Viewpoint structural audit](05-viewpoint-structural-audit.md) | Maps a thesis across sections and compresses relabeled frameworks |
| [Taxonomy preservation](06-taxonomy-preservation.md) | Negative control: retains categories with distinct decision consequences |
| [World English preservation](07-world-english.md) | Improves clarity without replacing a legitimate local academic register |

The two structural cases are also represented as machine-readable expectations in [`tests/behavior_cases.json`](../tests/behavior_cases.json).

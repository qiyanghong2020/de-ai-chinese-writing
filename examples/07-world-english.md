# World English: edit for clarity without erasing register

**Case type:** English academic discussion
**Source status:** Synthetic demonstration
**Goal:** Improve clarity while preserving a legitimate setting-specific authorial voice.

## Input

> In our setting, follow-up does not always mean that the patient returns to the same hospital. Many families continue care in district facilities after the first visit, particularly when travel cost is high. Therefore, an absence in our hospital record should not straightaway be taken as discontinuation of care. This point is important while interpreting the six-month retention estimate.

## Protected meaning and voice

- setting-specific care pathway
- some families continue at district facilities
- travel cost is relevant
- missing records at the study hospital do not necessarily mean discontinued care
- the limitation applies to the six-month retention estimate
- the author's direct phrase “in our setting” is useful and should remain

## Diagnosis

The paragraph is already specific and authorial. “Straightaway” and “while interpreting” are legitimate South Asian English forms, not proof of poor writing or AI use. Only the last two sentences need a slightly tighter relationship.

## Revision

> In our setting, follow-up does not always mean that the patient returns to the same hospital. Many families continue care at district facilities after the first visit, particularly when travel costs are high. An absence from our hospital record should therefore not be read automatically as discontinuation of care, a limitation that affects the six-month retention estimate.

## What was not normalized

The revision keeps “in our setting,” the local care pathway, and the paragraph's direct stance. It does not replace the passage with impersonal global-academic phrasing or add a claim about how often transfers occur.

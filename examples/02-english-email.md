# English workplace email: make the ask visible

**Case type:** English workplace email
**Source status:** Synthetic demonstration
**Goal:** Keep a courteous professional tone while removing generic setup and repeated thanks.

## Input

> Subject: A gentle follow-up regarding the revised figures
>
> Dear Maya,
>
> I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to kindly follow up regarding the revised figures that we discussed during our meeting earlier this week. As you may recall, these figures will play an important role in helping us finalize the presentation in a timely and effective manner. If it is convenient for you, would it perhaps be possible to share the revised files by 3:00 p.m. on Friday? This would allow me to complete the slide deck before Monday's review. I sincerely appreciate your valuable time and continued support, and I would like to thank you once again for your kind assistance.
>
> Best regards,
> Arun

## Protected details

- recipient: Maya
- requested item: revised figures
- deadline: Friday at 3:00 p.m.
- reason: complete the slide deck before Monday's review
- relationship: professional and courteous

## Diagnosis

The actual request appears halfway through the message. The opening, the sentence about the figures' “important role,” and the double thanks do not add context or soften a difficult request.

## Revision

> Subject: Revised figures by Friday
>
> Dear Maya,
>
> Could you send the revised figures by 3:00 p.m. on Friday? I need them to finish the slide deck before Monday's review.
>
> Thanks for your help.
>
> Best,
> Arun

## Why this works

The revised email is still polite, but the recipient can see the item, deadline, and reason immediately. It does not invent urgency or change the requested time.

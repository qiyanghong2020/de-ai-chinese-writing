## Purpose

The skill provides editorial instructions for revising Chinese and English prose. It is designed to improve clarity, specificity, naturalness, and document structure while preserving facts, numbers, citations, uncertainty, genre, and author intent.

It does not determine whether text was written by a person or a model, guarantee an AI-detector result, bypass disclosure requirements, or replace professional editorial, legal, medical, academic, or institutional review.

## User responsibilities

By using the skill, you agree to:

- use only text that you are authorized to process;
- protect confidential, unpublished, patient-related, legally privileged, and commercially sensitive information;
- review all outputs before relying on, publishing, submitting, or sharing them;
- verify facts, citations, quotations, numerical values, and evidence claims independently;
- follow the policies of the journal, employer, institution, platform, or other setting in which the text will be used; and
- avoid using the skill to fabricate evidence, impersonate another writer, conceal misconduct, or misrepresent authorship or AI involvement.
